# Quest 3S VR Experience

The scene includes natural head tracking, Touch controller movement and interaction, snap turning, flight, a mild comfort vignette, lantern and gate interactions, and confirmation sounds. No ring controller is needed.

## Implemented experience

| Feature | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Spatial audio | Wind, water, candles, and ancestors have positioned sources; the interior hall has mild reverb. |
| Physical interactions | Touch controllers can pick up lanterns, light candles, and push the stone gate. Distinct short haptic pulses acknowledge actions. |
| Responsive environment | Banners and flames move gently; flight through clouds adds sparse particles and wind. The camera remains stable. |
| Comfort | Optional teleport, seated height calibration, speed adjustment, snap turning, and a gentle vignette are available. |

Quest 3S frame rate, comfort, and voice recognition still require testing on the headset. See the English controls guide for exact inputs.

References: [Meta XR Audio SDK](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/meta-xr-audio-sdk-unity/), [Meta Haptics SDK](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-haptics-sdk/), and [Meta Unity rendering guidance](https://developers.meta.com/vr/documentation/unity/unity-rendering/).
